01 · Circus

Five seasons on the same stage

I trained at Circo para Todos, the national circus school of Colombia, and have worked professionally in Europe since 2018. Since 2022 I have performed five consecutive seasons at Leolandia, Italy, with the company Circo e Dintorni: daily stage shows, parades and themed events for family audiences, about ten months a year.

I work in ensemble shows and as a soloist, in character and in costume, on large stages and in close contact with the public.

02 · Light & technology

I build my own light

LED props

Poi, buugeng and sticks with addressable LEDs, designed in 3D, printed, soldered and programmed by me. The props can draw images in the air while they spin (persistence of vision) and can be controlled live over WiFi, in sync with the music.

Buflow is the small Colombian brand that grew out of this work.

Light painting

Long exposures of my poi with a Fuji X-T3 on a tripod. The same gesture that disappears on stage stays on the photograph.

A live system

In TouchDesigner I connect the body, sound and light: a Kinect camera reads my movement, the music drives the props and room lights, and a diffusion model running on my computer turns the image of my body into a projection, live and offline.

This is the work I bring to art and technology residencies.

04 · About

From the traffic lights to the stage

Before the stages there were the traffic lights: years juggling at red lights in Medellín, waiting for those forty seconds to step out and do a trick. I didn't know that light would lead me to study light in other ways, first capturing it and then drawing with it.

Photography came in 2010, film in 2013, and circus school soon after. Since graduating in 2017 I have worked only as a circus artist: a first international contract in Italy in 2018, and since 2022 long seasons in Europe.

The same obsession led me to build my own light props: I learned 3D design, electronics and firmware until the first poi, big and rough, finally worked. That is how Buflow started.

Available for seasons, shows, events, workshops and collaborations.
